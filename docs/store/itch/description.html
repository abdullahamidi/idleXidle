The fight runs itself. The choices are yours.
Your champion stands in the arena and fights wave after wave — on every screen, and for hours after you close the game. Every wave it clears pays out at once. Every fifth is a boss. When it falls, it gets back up and starts a new descent. The new descent begins at the first wave, or at the checkpoint you picked on the Map. A checkpoint costs MEMORY DUST every time a descent starts there. What the champion already earned stays yours.

You never swing the sword. You watch, you read what the fight is telling you, and you decide what the champion becomes. One skill at a time, one cast at a time — a fast build casts fast, and you can see every cast land.

Twelve shared skills across six styles — HAMMER crushes, VOLLEY rains, SNARE answers, SIGN amplifies, FIELD ticks, DRAIN feeds — plus one signature skill for each champion. Every skill grows its own variations and reinforcements as it levels from use. Add Vows — rules you accept for extra power that only pay while your build keeps the rule — and Keystones that trade one thing for another. Then a Mastery tree of four directions decides how your champion fights.

Every item shows its real numbers. The Forge upgrades, re-rolls, sockets gems and salvages. It always asks before it sockets or crushes a gem. It asks before it sells or salvages, unless you switch that question off. RE-ROLL and MERGE happen at once, with no question. From the sixth UPGRADE on, an upgrade can slip and drop the item one level and one step, and the button shows the chance first. Weapons and armour belong to one of five classes, and a champion wears only its own class. Rings, charms and focus items fit everyone. Gear for another class waits for that class's champions, or becomes fuel for the Forge.

Six regions, each clearly tougher than the last. Hold wave twenty in a region and it is conquered. Past that line, the banner counts OVERWAVE: how far past wave twenty this descent has gone. Every ten waves you have held becomes a checkpoint you can start from, paid in MEMORY DUST. Once all six regions are yours, the Corruption opens on the Map. It has five steps. Each step DEEPER brings richer rewards, and tougher enemies that wear the Corruption. Press SHALLOWER when it bites.

Ten champions in five classes, each with an always-on passive that reshapes your build. You start as THE SEEKER, the first WANDERER. Each other class sends its first champion when you conquer its region. CINDERWORKS, UMBRAL REACH, MARROW WASTES and THE STILL ARCHIVE each bring one. The second of every class is earned: reach wave fifty in CINDERWORKS, keep a Vow through three whole descents, clear a hundred and fifty waves with a VOLLEY skill equipped, hold wave eighty in MARROW WASTES, fell forty bosses.

Facilities make GLEAM, MEMORY DUST and forge materials while you play, and while you are away. New facilities open as you conquer. At first, the game pays for up to two hours away. Every facility level you buy adds a little more time, up to twelve hours. Come back, spend, leave. The Warren opens once your first region is conquered.

Twenty-six traits open from what you have actually done — how you fight, what you survive, where you go — and they are never bought. Each hunter wears three of them, and changing which three costs nothing at all.

Alpha — what to expect

  • In: the whole loop — six regions, ten champions, the forge, the mastery tree, the traits, the warren, offline progress, save and load.
  • Rough: balance past wave forty, some animations, the sound mix. Numbers will move between builds.
  • Not planned: multiplayer, leaderboards, a market. This is an offline game and stays one.
  • Feedback: during play, press Escape (or the gear at the top right) to open SETTINGS, then press COPY FEEDBACK CODE. Paste the code in the comments with a sentence about what happened. The code holds the game version, your champion's skills, keystones and gear, and how far you got. It holds nothing personal.
  • Windows only for now. The exe is not signed, so Windows may warn you on first launch. Press More info, then Run anyway.
