Built by `tools/asset-pipeline/make_brand_cues.py` (spec: `design/audio/seeker-brand-audio-brief.md`). Every excerpt here
is a real CC0 1.0 recording; `SOURCES.md` (generated by `--extract`) lists each one with its page. Nothing is

## One family: three parts in every cue

| part | what it is made of | A SUBTLE / INTERNAL | B SUPERNATURAL | C ORGANIC / ASH |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TEXTURE (the curse's breath) | `suck_pipe`, `air_draw`, `air_exhale`, `steam`, `fire_steam`, `sand_pour`, `dust_fall` | suction + bellows air drawn in, bellows air let out | a REVERSED steam hiss and air pulled in; a high fluttering hiss let out | a fire's steam tightening; sand and dust pouring out |
| RESONANCE (the low internal body) | `drum_res` (pitched down), `body_thud`, `body_thud_b`, `tube_hit`, `body_hit` | one voice (-4 st), a muffled thud + the body's give | two voices a quarter-tone apart that beat (corrupted), a reversed swell INTO the hold, a hollow tube | one brighter voice (-3 st), thud + a close tissue hit |
| DRY (the corruption) | `leather_crawl`, `paper_burn`, `leaf_crush`, `twig_snap`, `twig_snap_b`, `salt_grain`, `cloth_scrape`, `stone_scrape`, `gas_puff` | a leather creak's grains, kept low | burning-paper grains REVERSED (each swells into its tick) | burning-paper crackle and leaf crush, a strong brittle snap |

## The seven cues (layers in order)

- **apply** (take-hold at 180 ms): lead-in (A suction + air / B reversed hiss + reversed resonance / C steam + crackle
  crowding in) -> the take-hold (thud + resonance + snap) -> tail (texture out + thinning dry grains).
- **deepen** (230 ms): wake 0-80 (a few quiet dry grains) -> crawl 0-240 (A a muffled cloth/stone scrape climbing 260 ->
  900 Hz / B a hiss band gliding 0.9 -> 3.6 kHz, fluttering / C a leather creak crawling) -> take-hold -> short tail.
- **deepen_deep** (230 ms): deepen + one extra dry layer after the hold + more low-mid body (a second thud 120-700 Hz),
  a longer resonance and tail; mastered 0.5 dB under apply, never louder.
- **infect** (15 ms): the take-hold alone (thud, a short resonance, the snap), a little texture out; no lead-in.
- **leave** (10 ms): flare (A an air puff / B a steam flash / C a crackle burst) + a small thud -> an inward collapse
  (the family's lead-in shape, 110-380 ms; B's with a reversed resonance) -> an exhale (texture out) to the end.
- **awaken** (120 ms): an internal inhale (the lead-in shape, 0-120) -> take-hold -> short tail.
- **ash** (5 ms): a twig's brittle crack with a small low knock under it -> a dust crumble (from 45 ms) -> sparse salt
  grains -> a dusty exhale (B: a hiss).

Every cue is mastered to its brief loudness (K-weighted loudest 50 ms x its `MarkRecipe` volume x 0.8), true peak
<= 0.37, a 1 ms fade in and a 45 ms squared release; every layer has its own release.
