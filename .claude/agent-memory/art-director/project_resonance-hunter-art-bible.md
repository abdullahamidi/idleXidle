The Resonance Hunter art bible lives at `design/art/art-bible.md` (9-section skeleton created 2026-07-13, status Draft). Sections are drafted one at a time per the project's incremental-file-writing convention (see `.claude/docs/context-management.md`). Game facts (pillars, Sources, automation stages, MVP scope) live in `design/gdd/game-concept.md` — re-read it rather than trusting this memory's paraphrase, since it changes independently of the art bible.

**Sections 1–4 confirmed written to file as of 2026-07-13** (verified by reading `design/art/art-bible.md` directly): Visual Identity Statement, Mood & Atmosphere, Shape Language, Color System. Load-bearing decisions worth knowing before touching later sections:
- Master rule: every mechanically important state renders as a distinct glyph, never a color swap alone.
- Glyph inversion device: a boss's vulnerability glyph heals/inverts into its ward-seal at the Mastery Transition — same symbol, flipped meaning. Light must expand outward/upward at that beat, never contract (contraction reads as loss).
- Contrast Mechanism (S2/S3.3, locked): Unconquered and Mastered share **identical glyph geometry and scar placement** — only integrity, color temperature, and light behavior change. "The crack never moves, it only closes."
- S3.1/4.2 locked a two-axis creature-identity grammar: Role reads through mass distribution, Source (body/mind/nature/machine/shadow/spirit) reads through edge quality + a dedicated color per Source — color is always secondary/reinforcing, never the sole signal (colorblind-safety audit table exists in 4.6).
- S3.5 locked a per-frame hero-shape hierarchy for combat (vulnerability glyph > creature silhouette > Source glyph/seams > arena background) and established that environmental ornament is suppressed during live combat, blooming only in idle/establishing shots.
- S4.4 Temperature Law: conquest-state color (cool-unconquered / warm-mastered) is an establishing-shot/idle-view signal only — suppressed to Cold Slate neutral during live combat regardless of actual conquest state.
- Section 5 (Character Design Direction) still owes: Pillar 3's idle-creature "still working" animation signal (seed language: "work-loop glyph pulse," S2), and Pillar 4's hunter-silhouette Vow treatment (seed language: "searing/scarring," matching the Forge's item-side Vow scar, S2).

**Section 6 (Environment Design Language) — drafted 2026-07-13 by this agent, handed back as markdown for approval; NOT yet written to file.** Non-obvious calls made, worth preserving even after the section is written (rationale isn't always visible from the file text alone):
- **Two compositions per region, not one**: every region needs both a tight "Arena" composition (combat, single-screen, suppressed per 3.5/4.4) and a wide "Region View" composition (idle/automated view + region overview, full mood/color-temperature/infrastructure). Same bones, two camera contexts — this resolves how a "contained arena, no free-roam" game still supports the idle/automated wide view Section 2 requires.
- **Region identity axis = dominant elemental Source** (reusing 3.1/4.2's edge-quality + color grammar at environment scale), NOT an independent biome system and NOT unique per-region architecture. Biome falls out as a consequence of Source, not a separate design axis. All regions share one underlying "ward-network ruin" architecture language as connective tissue, so they read as fragments of one shattered system rather than unrelated tilesets. Chosen over biome-first and architecture-first for predictive legibility (Pillar 5) and production efficiency (no second identity system to invent).
- **Procedural vs. hand-crafted recommendation** (this directly resolves the open question flagged in `game-concept.md`, "Is region content hand-crafted, or procedural?"): recommended terrain/architecture/sigil-scar crack-path geometry be 100% hand-crafted, once per region. Reasoning: Section 3.3's "identical crack path in both states" rule is not just a quality preference procedural gen does worse at — it's a hard compatibility constraint procedural regeneration would violate outright. Procedural technique is only recommended for non-mechanical decorative scatter and ambient motion timing. Flagged for `technical-artist`/`producer` to confirm against actual pipeline tooling and final region count (TBD, resolved at `/map-systems`).
- **Automation infrastructure is additive, cumulative, and staged** to match the four automation unlocks in `game-concept.md` (auto-attack → auto-loot → auto-craft/sell → offline progression): ward-post → collection cairn/hopper → forge-stall → tall always-lit beacon (sized to read at region-select thumbnail scale). All four stack visibly in a fully-progressed region so automation stage is legible without a menu (Principle 3).
- **Recurring "empty plinth" motif**: a small ward-alcove scattered through every region, dark/cracked when unconquered, lit with a small Hearth-Gold flame-glyph when mastered — reuses the crack-treatment grammar at miniature scale rather than inventing new geometry, gives many small "did I do this" reward beats instead of only the one big transition, and doubles as environmental storytelling (the world already implied sockets waiting to be filled before automation ever plugs into them).
