# Blind reads, second pass (2026-09-27)

Fresh readers, one image each, neutral file names, no knowledge of the design. Two kinds of test: STATIC shape
reads (the brief's own method for the motif) and every-frame flipbooks at half size (weak for motion, as in every
battery here).

## The whelp: key-pose rows at play size, with a travel arrow (`05b`, `05c`)

| Row | Verb | Contact frame | Frame 4 alone | Sequence 1–5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| warped strip (rejected) | lunging, low–medium | frame 4 | "lunge/impact, head-first horizontal reach" | 2 |
| puppet, first draft | lunging, moderate | frame 4 | "impact/lunge; rearing would need the head raised, not below" | 2.5 |
| puppet, final (coil deepened, commit lowered) | lunging, ~45 % | frame 4 | "impact/lunge; head down and forward, not raised" | 2 |

Every row: the CONTACT pose reads as impact / lunge and never as rearing; the sequence scores low on paper because
the readers see the coil and commit as "near-identical" or "a bob". The final draft moved the coil further back and
down and put the commit's head between the coil and the contact so the head height no longer oscillates; the reader
of that row still called frames 1 and 3 similar. Root-off flipbook (`blind/clip_p1.png`): "a crouch–rise cycle,
mostly up and down in place", 2/5. This is the honest paper state; the true-speed film `02` is the test.

## The motif: static cards, the closed state on the target alone, 2× (`06`, `07`)

| Card | Shape as seen | First word | Alternatives | Two things closed? | "Bitten" |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 opposing crescents | "a ring broken into two facing crescents, tips pointing at one centre" | clamp | bite, pinch | yes, "consistent with jaws" | 2 |
| M2 two-fang snap | "two wedges stacked vertically, tips meeting at a white dot" | pinch | clamp, bite | yes | 2 |
| M3 serrated clamp | "two crescents with dots, separated" | clamp | bite, pinch | yes, "two distinct points of contact" | 2 |
| M4 hybrid (selected) | "two arc/wedge shapes like an hourglass, meeting at a white-hot dot" | clamp | pinch, bite | yes | 2 |

Gate: bite / chomp / snap / clamp wanted, slash / scratch / claw disqualifying. All four pass; none is called
slash. None reaches "bitten" above 2, and every reader gives the same reason: nothing tooth- or mouth-like is drawn.
The brief forbids a cartoon mouth, so this is the ceiling of an abstract mark; the lunge supplies the rest.

## Flipbooks at half size, the whole fight in frame (`blind/`)

| Clip | What it is | What was read | Clarity |
|---|---|---|---|
| p1 | authored strip, root OFF, everything off | a crouch–rise cycle in place | 2/5 |
| p2 | the full hit (lunge + motif + flash + number) | the creatures "never move"; on his chest "a flat red bowtie/hourglass icon, reads as a UI status icon" | 1/5 |
| p3 | rough A2 at fast tempo | the motif "a red hourglass icon on his chest"; the Shadow jaws "thin purple thorny tendrils" on the creatures; the cause "his own sword thrust" | 1/5 |

What these say: flipbook readers do not see the 67 px lunge or the two-frame snap, exactly as they did not see the 80 px
lunge, the 27 px recoil or the maw's shut in earlier batteries; but two of them, unprompted, called the closed motif
an "hourglass icon" at play size. That is a real risk for the shape: symmetric and centred, it can read as a glyph. If
the owner sees the same at true speed, the fix is asymmetry (the jaws rooted on the enemy's side) or a touch of the
enemy's own material, not more detail.
