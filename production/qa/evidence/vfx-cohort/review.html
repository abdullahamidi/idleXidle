Every generation, including the rejected ones

Decisions recorded (spec.json effects.archetypes, arena-art-contract.md)

  • Projectiles: two motion layers. The runtime flies the strip; the strip itself must visibly change. A translated static picture is not approved.
  • Impact assembly: input frame + generated 1–7 when an open-ended impact ends empty (fxclips.py --impact-from-input, refused for anything held).
  • PRESS: an abstract line-art pressure field. The square weight is not approved and was reverted.
  • Traps: activation must read as appear, then tighten, then hold; after that a trap may stay still. The Seeker snare installed in this cohort appears already tight, so it does not yet show the tighten cue.
  • Trap frames: keep the 512 contract. On the widest row the budget clamp already draws the trap slightly smaller.
