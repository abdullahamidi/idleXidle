Decisions for you

  • Assembly rule for impacts: assemble open-ended impacts from the input frame plus generated 1–7. It removes the most common failure (an empty last frame: 3 of the 8 open-ended dispersal clips across all three rounds) and would rescue the anvil for free. The approved Seeker strike is unaffected unless it is refiled.
  • PRESS field picture: keep the square weight, or redraw it as a line-art sigil like the other fields.
  • Subtle motion: accept a knife that flies without spinning and a snare that appears already tight. PixelLab does not move rigid objects.
  • Widest-row traps: 1.31 over budget even when the art fills its width. Options: a wider native frame, or a smaller trap scale.
