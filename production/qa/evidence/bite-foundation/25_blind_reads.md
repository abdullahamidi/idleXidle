# Blind reads on the new foundation (2026-09-26)

**Method.** As in the two studies: fresh subagents, one clip each, neutral names in a neutral folder, every frame
of a ~1 s window at 60 fps as a half-size flipbook (the reflected number and the skill bar cropped out), "answer as
after one viewing at full speed". Two rounds: round 1 on the first build (recoil 5 %, the acceptance take still
carrying the flash, the softer coil), round 2 on the filed build (recoil 6 % with the dip, the acceptance take with
the flash off, the deeper coil). **A subagent cannot watch video**: a flipbook is more forgiving than true speed for
a still, and less forgiving for MOTION (a 16 px shift across two half-size frames is 8 px on paper). The two rounds
disagree on the same content, which is the instrument's noise; the true-speed films are the owner's test.

## Reading it

- Compared with the two earlier studies (no reader ever saw the creature act), the lunge was seen by three of the
  ten readers here ("popped forward", "lunged with a purple arc", "stretched forward a touch"), and A got, once,
  the sentence the whole exercise is after: "it bit him and got something back for it… automatic". The other
  reads did not get there.
- Nobody saw the Seeker's body react in either round, at 5 % or 6 % of his width with the dip.
- The readers' repeated words for what is missing are "no snap, no contact", and for the coil, "duck down". The
  strip's contact pose and the lunge's arrival at extension are one frame apart; on paper that is one frame.
- The flipbook's own noise is large: the same A concept read "caused, automatic" in one round and "cast" in the
  other, over footage whose only differences were the recoil's size and the coil's depth.
