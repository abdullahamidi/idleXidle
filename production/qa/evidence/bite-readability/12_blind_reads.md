# Blind reads (2026-09-26)

**Method.** Ten fresh subagents, one clip each, in a scratch folder whose path names nothing. Each read one image
with a neutral name (`clip_p2.png` …): EVERY frame of a ~1 s window at 60 fps in reading order, at half size,
cropped to y 522..900 so the words that name a skill (the reflected number, the skill bar) are out of view. They
were told only that the hooded figure is the player's champion, the dark creatures are enemies, the fight plays
itself, and to "play it in your head at that speed" and answer as after ONE viewing at full speed.

**Limitation, stated plainly.** The owner asked for TRUE-SPEED VIDEO reads. A subagent cannot watch a video; the
every-frame flipbook is the closest it gets, and it is MORE forgiving than true speed (a reader can dwell on a
frame). "I never saw it" from a reader who could dwell is therefore a strong negative; "I saw it" is a weak
positive. The true-speed films are on the review page for the owner's own eye, which is the real test.
