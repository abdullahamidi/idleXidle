Two independent panels, three judges scoring every design of both, one synthesis, one adversarial
verification, one repair against its 24 defects, and one re-verification. What follows is the
repaired design plus the four adjudications below it, which are mine and are final.

## The four blocking defects the re-verification found, and what I decided

The verifier was right about all four. Two dials go; two designs get a content fix. That takes the
new-dial count from ten to eight, which is nearer the six the synthesis was asked for.

**1. `FirstPulseAtWaveStart` is deleted, and GROUNDWORK with it.** The dial edited the field arrears
gate's `ms == 0` clause, and `ms` never takes the value 0 — the tick loop opens at `TickMs` (100), so
that clause is vestigial dead code from an older loop. A reinforcement whose condition cannot be true
is the dormant-feature bug this project is named for. BREASTWORK's third purchase becomes
**FOOTING**: `WaveStartShieldFraction`, which is live and is what SNARE/BANKED/CARRIED already reads —
the wave opens with the plate already on. Same sentence, no new dial, and a site that fires.

**2. `SwingOverkillCarry` is deleted, and THROUGH GRAIN with it.** The specified edit left
`impactSwing` and `castCarry` set across the whole of the swing's LandSpread, so the carried hit could
carry again — a chain, where the fight's own comment at the spill block says "one level only" and the
card promises one. Bounding it needs a re-entry flag threaded through the hottest method in the sim
for one reinforcement. OPEN HAND's third purchase becomes **SLOW HANDS**: the blow comes round every
eight beats instead of six, and the hands land harder still (`CooldownMultiplier` plus the branch's
own `SwingPower`, both live). It is the branch's own trade stated once more — give the cast away, take
the beats back — and the swing is the action that fills a beat no skill claims.

**3. CHANTRY's numbers change so HUSH can bind.** HUSH raised a cap the wave can never reach: the bite
read is `max(1 - cap, 1 - perCorpse * dead)`, and at 0.10 a corpse the cap at 0.45 needs five corpses —
but the fifth death ends the wave through `Kill(ms)` before any bite is read, so four is the most that
can ever be counted. CHANTRY's base cap drops to 0.25, which binds at three corpses, and HUSH raises it
to 0.45. Same purchase, now reachable inside a real wave.

**4. The Kill dispatch must exclude WEEP, and a test must say so.** BACKDRAW revives
`ReactionOn.Kill`, which is declared and never dispatched — except that WEEP declares it too, and is
answered by the bleed scan instead. A dispatch written as the mirror of the Bitten gate would sweep
WEEP in: a skill event and a projectile on every corpse, twice per death. The gate is
`Kind == Reaction && On == ReactionOn.Kill && BasePower > 0f`, WEEP has no BasePower, and a test
asserts that a WEEP build emits no skill event on a kill.

## The nine minor defects become a test, not a paragraph

All nine are the same shape: a `weakOnOther` claim that is overstated or inverted, because a
reinforcement writes an absolute value onto the resolved definition and therefore often works fine on
the sibling variation. Rewriting nine paragraphs would produce nine more claims nobody can check.
Instead the implementation carries a test: every reinforcement is measured in a real fight on BOTH
variations of its own skill, and must be worth materially more on the one it belongs to. That turns
the design's weakest promise into the suite's strongest guarantee, and it generalises to the sixty
reinforcements the twelve shipped skills already have.

## The designs in full

### THE SEEKER — HARD HANDS (`sig_seeker_hard_hands`)

**Hammer · Active.** You never learned a school, so you fight the way you walked: with your hands, over and over, for as long as it takes.

**Base line.** Heavy damage to one target every 6 beats. While it is woven, every basic attack you make lands 70% harder.

**Dials.** Beats 6 — read at SoloBattle.cs:1661 `var beats = sk.Def.Beats;`, inside the cast block that begins at :1655 `if (!onBeat || acted) continue;`. Targets 1 — :1940 `var spreadTargets = shape.TargetsFor(vdef, vdef.Targets) + vdef.TargetsBonus;`. BasePower 400 — :1818 `raw = SkillCatalogue.PoweredBase(sk.Def, resonance);`. NEW Rules.SwingPower = 0.70f — gathered in the existing per-wave weave loop at :764-768 beside SwingIgnoresArmour (:766) and SwingLifesteal (:767), read at ONE site as one extra factor inside the basic-attack block :2109-2126, on the LandSpread raw at :2117-2118. NEW Rules.SwingOverkillCarry = 0f at the base line — same gather block, spent at :2115-2116.

**Why this character.** The starter is the only champion with Lean = null and no affinity, and its passive is deliberately the dullest in the roster. The basic attack is the action the champion takes on every beat no skill claims — at a six-beat signature and a five-beat shared active that is roughly three beats in five — and nothing in the twelve shared skills is built on it: SwingIgnoresArmour is declared at SkillCatalogue.cs:291 and set by NO shipped skill, and SwingLifesteal is a third of one reinforcement. The champion with no discipline is the champion whose own hands are the build.

**How it meets the innate.** EVEN HAND is SkillShape.HitSize = 1.08 (CharacterRoster.cs:61), read at SoloBattle.cs:998 — two lines BELOW `if (skillDef is null) return m;` at :996. The swing calls LandSpread(..., null, null, ...) at :2117-2119, so Amp() returns at :996 and the innate provably never touches it. REPAIRED CLAIM: EVEN HAND is not the only innate behind that gate — MANY MOUTHS (:1029) and MOMENTUM (:1018) are behind it too. It is the only innate behind that gate that is UNCONDITIONAL: a flat HitSize at :998 with no creature-state, crowd or ordering condition. So it is the only one whose partition with the swing has no overlap in ANY wave shape — the innate owns every skill hit whatever the wave looks like, the signature owns every swing, and neither can restate the other in any composition.

**Presentation.** ClipKey "strike", FxKey "strike". HuntScreen.FxFor (:3662-3666) already prefers fx_seeker_strike_strip8_512 over the shared strip, so the art is a pure asset task with no code risk. The cast should read as one plain committed punch with no weapon in it. The important half of the presentation is the SWING: a swing carries FromSkill: false and HuntScreen grades its damage numeral a size down, which is correct everywhere except on a build where Rules.SwingPower > 0f and the swing IS the payload — that is one screen rule keyed on the dial, not new art. On THROUGH GRAIN the carried spill should draw as a short low follow-through from the corpse into the next creature, the same picture the Anvil's innate gets.

#### OPEN HAND — The blow gives its weight to your hands: it deals a third as much, and your basic attacks land 210% harder. *(Body)*

*Dials:* DamageMultiplier 1.0 → 0.35 (:1904 `raw *= vdef.DamageMultiplier`). Rules.SwingPower 0.70 → 2.10 (gathered :764-768, read :2117-2118).

- **BROAD KNUCKLE** — Your basic attacks land 330% harder instead of 210%.

  - *On the sibling:* STRUCTURALLY DEAD, and now provably so. SHUT FIST sets Rules.SwingPower = 0f outright, and this reinforcement multiplies rather than sets — 0 × 1.57 = 0, so the added factor `(1f + swingPower)` at :2117 resolves to 1 and not one number moves. - **SPLIT GRAIN** — Your basic attacks ignore defence.
  - *Dials:* SwingIgnoresArmour = true — declared at SkillCatalogue.cs:291, gathered at SoloBattle.cs:766, spent at the swing's `ignoresArmour: swingIgnoresArmour || trailArmed` argument at :2120. - **THROUGH GRAIN** — When a basic attack kills, half of its wasted damage carries into the next enemy.
  - *Dials:* NEW Rules.SwingOverkillCarry = 0.50f — gathered in the same :764-768 loop as `swingPower`, spent at :2115-2116 where the swing block already writes `impactSwing` and `castCarry`: `impactSwing = impact || swingCarry > 0f; castCarry = MathF.Max(impact ? 1f : 0f, swingCarry);`. SHUT FIST's swing sits at SwingPower 0 — the smallest hit the build makes — so `spill = -target.Health` at :1270 is almost never positive on it: a bare auto-attack rarely kills, and rarer still by enough to waste. OPEN HAND's swing is 3.1x and is the killing hit. Not structurally dead (a bool-and-float pair the sibling never zeroes), but a fraction of a level's worth.

### THE ANVIL — HARDFACE (`sig_anvil_hardface`)

**Hammer · Active.** You do not chase the wave. You set it on the anvil, and every one that goes down leaves the next one softer.

**Base line.** Heavy damage to one target every 6 beats. While it is woven, every enemy that dies — however it died — strips 12 defence from every enemy still standing, for the rest of the wave, down to -48.

**Dials.** Beats 6 (:1661). Targets 1 (:1940). BasePower 470 (:1818). DefenceBreakFloor -48f — the existing dial, reused as the clamp; no second floor is added. NEW Rules.DefenceBreakOnKill = 12f — read at ONE site: SoloBattle.cs:1246, the last statement inside the weave walk `for (var k = 0; k < skills.Count; k++)` opened at :1233, AFTER the `if (wd.BleedOnKillFraction > 0f)` block closes at :1245. THE CARD SAYS WHAT THE CODE DOES: that walk sits inside LandOn's death section and its own comment at :1224-1228 says "Read here rather than in the skill loop so ANY kill pays: a cast, the plain swing, a carried overkill or the bleed itself" — so the strip fires on every death from any source, and the base line is written that way rather than as "every enemy IT kills".

**Why this character.** THE ANVIL 'hits things until they are a different shape,' and its stated structural weakness is a crowd. HARDFACE is the other half of that sentence made mechanical: what goes down stays counted, and the wave it has been working is measurably softer than the wave it started on. It is the only skill in the game that produces negative armour from an Active rather than from a two-second field tick, and WaveCreature.Defense is `{ get; set; }` (SoloBattle.cs:159) for exactly this kind of lasting strip.

**How it meets the innate.** DEADWEIGHT is SkillShape.OverkillCarry = 0.33 (CharacterRoster.cs:78), and the fight takes `var carry = MathF.Max(shape.OverkillCarry, castCarry);` at :1271 — deliberately the LARGER of the two, never the sum. So a signature that carried overkill would be redundant on this champion below 0.33 and a rounding error above it. HARDFACE therefore USES the innate rather than repeating it, and it uses it in the direction the read site actually runs: because the strip pays on ANY death, the innate's carried spill at :1274-1276 is itself a strip-maker — the spill kills, the death strips every survivor, and negative armour makes the NEXT spill land harder because :1183 `else if (armour < 0f) dmg += MathF.Min(-armour, dmg);` adds the break back into the hit. Kill, strip, spill harder, kill again — and the Anvil is the only champion whose innate closes that loop without a cast. On anyone else the same skill is a blow with a bookkeeping clause that only its own kills feed.

**Presentation.** ClipKey "strike", FxKey "strike"; fx_anvil_strike_strip8_512. Not a swing but a DROP: the effect lands from above onto the creature and the shockwave spreads sideways along the ground through the whole row, so the wave-wide armour strip is something the player watches happen. The existing BattleEventKind.Break badge already draws over each broken creature, so a strip lights every survivor at once with no new event — and because it fires on any death, the badge must be allowed to light on a death the champion did not cause, which is exactly what the card now promises. The innate's carried spill should be drawn as a short low shockwave running from each corpse into the next living creature — the picture DEADWEIGHT has never had.

### THE CHORUS — GRAVE SONG (`sig_chorus_grave_song`)

**Field · Field.** The charms do not stop singing when something dies. They get louder.

**Base line.** Damages every enemy every 2s, and deals 35% more for each enemy the wave has already lost.

**Dials.** Kind Field with IntervalMs 2000 — read at :1472 `var ownTick = sk.Def.IntervalMs > 0 ? sk.Def.IntervalMs : 1_000;`, scheduled by the arrears gate at :1482 `if (ms == 0 || ms % auraTick != 0) continue;`.
