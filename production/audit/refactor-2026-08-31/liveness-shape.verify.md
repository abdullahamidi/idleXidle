## Missed — things in this area the report does not mention

1. **GLUT's "No lifesteal" is false.** `FormBehaviour.Heals(Form.Transformation)` at `SoloBattle.cs:1721-1731` heals 12% of `dealt` on *every* DRINK cast; GLUT sets `Lifesteal = 0f` (`SkillCatalogue.cs:558`) but the Form-keyed heal is not a dial and cannot be switched off. Related: THIRST's line "your per-wave healing limit doubles with it" (`:548`) has no mechanism — `BudgetFor(champ.MaxHealth, siphon: …)` (`:637-638`, `HealTuning.cs:129-132`) is raised only by the SIPHON trigger.
2. **spec_mark is inert under SPEND.** `if (sk.Def.AmplifyMs > 0) window = sk.Def.AmplifyMs;` (`SoloBattle.cs:1518`) replaces the window *after* Linger (`:1507`) and `MarkWindowMultiplier` (`:1508`) were applied. SPEND / OVERSPEND / AFTERGLOW (`SkillCatalogue.cs:384,386,390`) set it, so the MARK SPECIALIST node (grants Linger + `MarkWindowMultiplier 1.3`, `MasteryCatalog.cs:442-443`) does nothing on a SPEND CALL's cast path; STEADY likewise (window → infinite).
3. **The stats page already drifted.** `StatsScreen.cs:307` omits `shape.AutoAttackDamage`, which the sim applies at `SoloBattle.cs:1763`. Claim 33 warns about the risk; the defect is live for RESONANT/LODE/Body-5pc builds.
4. **HUD mislabels PULSE/REPAY in an active slot.** `SoloExpeditionScreen.cs:2926` keys the cooldown ring on `FormBehaviour.CooldownBeats(s.Form)`, which is 0 for `Form.Aura`/`Form.Trap` (native passive, `FormBehaviour.cs:73-74`), so PULSE gets a *timed* ring against `BaseCooldownMs(Aura) = 1000` and REPAY against `8000`, while the sim counts 5 beats (`:1453`); `:3039-3049` labels them "ALWAYS ON" / "WHEN BITTEN". Same root cause as claim 5's wrong reasoning.
5. **Weaver echoes by legacy Form, not by Kind** (`SoloBattle.cs:1705-1716`): PRESS is echoed as a 500-base Strike, WEEP as a 215×2 Projectile, MIRE as a 12 Aura, WILT as a 260 Transformation, and REPAY (a damage Active) is skipped because `FiresOnBeingHit(Form.Trap)`. Any Kind/Effect-based rewrite is a behaviour change for five skills — decide it as a fix, not a refactor.
6. **`Affix.Warded` is dead**, not just its reader. `Bands.cs:48` declares it ("60% less from whichever Form dealt the most damage LAST wave"), `BandCycles.cs:93,99,111` place it in three bands, and no `Bands.*` helper (`:155-180`) or other code reads it — `grep -rnw Warded src tests` finds nothing else. `LastWaveTopForm` was its input; both halves are dormant. By the same grep `Entrenched`, `Legion` and `Hollow` (affix, not the region) also have no consumer — outside the dial scope, flagged for follow-up.
7. **JAWS's re-arm is a Form-table constant, and the text is wrong.** JAWS has `IntervalMs: 0` (`SkillCatalogue.cs:350`), so `BuildComposer.cs:179-180` seeds `CooldownMs` from `FormBehaviour.BaseCooldownMs(Trap)` = the hard-coded `8_000` (`FormBehaviour.cs:112`). The catalogue line says "Rearms every 3s" and IRON "rearms every 6s" (`:349,:362`) — neither has a dial; the Reaction path (`SoloBattle.cs:1907-1909`) runs at 8 s × `CooldownMultiplier`.
8. **Latent `int.MaxValue + TargetsBonus` overflow.** `shape.TargetsFor(form) + def.TargetsBonus` (`SoloBattle.cs:1424`) and `TargetsFor(form, vdef.Targets) + vdef.TargetsBonus` (`:1654`) wrap negative when the baseline is `WholeWave` (`SkillCatalogue.cs:255`), and `LandSpread` returns 0 for `targets <= 0` (`:1089`). No shipped variation combines them (PRESS/SEIZE `continue`s at `:1327`; SPLAY and PULSE have no `TargetsBonus` reinforcement), but a future "reaches one more" on a whole-wave skill would silently zero it.
9. **`SavedSkill` carries no `SkillId`** (`SaveGame.cs:294-311`; writers `Game1.cs:880,946`). The report's §8 remedy assumes the save already has one.
10. **`SaveGame.Affinity` (`:158`) is never written or read** — dead save field.
11. **Test fallout of the small deletions** is unlisted: `GearMods.Combine` → `GearTraitsTests.cs:170,205`; `RingDistance/Opposite` → `skill_catalogue_test.cs:148-160`; `AbilityPower` → `WeavingTests.cs:207-265`. The report names tests only for the 18 orphans.
12. **`Heals(form)` at `:1435` is unreachable** for the shipped catalogue (WILT `continue`s at `:1373`; only MIRE reaches the Field damage path); the report lists it among the live Form-keyed consumers.
13. **`FormBehaviour.IsPassive/FiresOnBeingHit` are the migration bridge** at `Build.cs:220` (`EquippedSkill.Passive` default) and `BuildComposer.cs:102-103` (`SlotKinds`); the §5 replacement table omits them, and a "delete FormBehaviour predicates" pass would break every pre-rework save's slot kinds.
