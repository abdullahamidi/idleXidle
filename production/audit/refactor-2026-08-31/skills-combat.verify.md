## Important things the auditor missed

1. **Replacing the Form hexagon with the Style ring is a balance change, not a refactor.** The Form enum order (`ResonanceWeaving.cs:9`) makes Strike↔Trap, Projectile↔Mark, Aura↔Transformation opposites (`FormBehaviour.cs:267-284`). The Style order (`SkillCatalogue.cs:20-33, 35-54`) makes Hammer↔Volley, Snare↔Field, Sign↔Drain opposites — by design "corrected". A Hammer adept's Snare skill goes from ×0.45 to ×1.15 the day `Build.Affinity` becomes `Style?`. Every affinity/family/balance-sweep test number moves; this needs a design sign-off, not a mechanical edit.
2. **In-session vs post-reload composition differ for a third Active.** `BuildComposer.SlotKinds :88-93` never spills a pick that carries a `SkillId` (it just `actives++`), and `Compose :189` ignores `Build.Weave`'s `false` (`Build.cs:453/461/463` refuse a third Active) — so a named third Active is **silently dropped**. `SkillId` is never persisted (`PlayerLoadout.cs:278-279`), so after reload the same loadout is Form-only and **spills** into the passive slot as its style's passive skill. Same build, two different fights.
3. **`Resolve(Form, bool)` is the hot path, not a bridge.** `EquippedSkill.Def` (`Build.cs:252-263`) re-runs two LINQ `First` scans over `All` plus `with`-copies of a 57-field record on every access; the tick loop reads `.Def` at 21 sites, ×4 skills × ~1200 ticks per wave. Storing the resolved Def on EquippedSkill is the architectural fix and a perf fix in one.
4. **The six Form-combo triggers have a second producer.** `MasteryCatalog.cs:434-446` Spec nodes grant Execute/Coiled/Overdraw/Radiance/Linger/Siphon. Re-keying `Enchantments.Needs` alone leaves the mastery grants — and their `Form`-typed node field — untouched.
5. **`ShareCodes.SharedBuild.Skills` (ShareCodes.cs:48) is a second durable (Form, Passive) format.** §13 lists only `SaveGame`. A share code minted today must still decode after Form is gone.
6. **`FormBehaviour.CastClipMs` is dead**, not "consumed only by Game" — no reader anywhere; its doc points at `SoloBattle.CastGapFor`, which does not exist.
7. **FormBehaviour has 14 Game-side read sites** (rail cooldown text, EVERY-Nth-ACTION label, clip speed, aura pulse constant, hex diagram, WeaveScreen affinity preview). The report's §3 table names three; the delete is a Game migration.
8. **Weaver echo turns a Field into a cast.** `:1702-1719` echoes `skills[(i+1)%n]` at its Form's base: a PRESS in the next slot fires `500 × 0.45` Strike damage per cast; MIRE/PULSE fire `12 × 0.45`. The report flags only REPAY's misclassification.
9. **`SoloBattle.SiphonLeechMultiplier` (:370) is test-only** (`heal_balance_test.cs:302`); the sim reads `heal.SiphonMultiplier` (:1437, :1729). One more constant to delete rather than re-key.
10. **`Build.cs:219-220` Passive fallback is production-unreachable** (the sole constructor passes `PassiveSlot`); only tests hit it.
11. **`SaveGame.Affinity` is a dead string** — never written past its default, never read. Drop it; it is not a Form.
12. **`SkillCatalogue.Taught` has no reader** (the `Taught.Everything()` in tests is a different helper) — safe to delete outright.
13. **SingleForm → SingleStyle is provably semantics-preserving** (Form↔Style bijection via LegacyForm), which the auditor could have stated to de-risk that edit.
